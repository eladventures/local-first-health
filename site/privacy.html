Privacy on this site.

This site has no trackers, no analytics, no cookies, and no forms. It loads nothing from third parties.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us by email to hello@localfirsthealth.org: your email address, your name if you include it, and what you write.

Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and pages requested, to run and protect the site. We do not use them to identify or follow visitors.

Why we use it

  • To read your message and respond as capacity allows.
  • To inform our thinking about LFH. We ask permission before naming or quoting you publicly and do not include patient information.

Emailing LFH does not subscribe you to updates. We do not sell your information, share it for marketing or add you to the sales lists of any commercial app.

Please don't send patient information

Describe the workflow, not a patient. Do not submit patient records or identifying medical details. If you send them by mistake, we will delete them.

How long we keep it

We keep messages only as long as we need them for LFH work. To ask us to delete what you sent, email hello@localfirsthealth.org with the subject "Delete my information".

The record should travel. Not the patient.
