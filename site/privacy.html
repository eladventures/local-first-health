Privacy on this site.

This site has no trackers, no analytics, no cookies and no forms. It makes no requests to third parties: its pages, fonts and images are all served from this site.

Offline reading

To let you read the site without a connection, the site uses a service worker. If your browser supports offline saving and finishes saving the site, you can read the saved pages without a connection. Your browser may later remove that copy. To save the site it downloads the pages, fonts and images in the background, so our host's logs may show those requests.

You can remove the saved copy at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

What we collect

When you email LFH, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. Email is the only way to contact LFH.

Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and pages requested, to run and protect the site. We do not use them to identify or follow visitors.

Why we use it

  • To read your message and respond as capacity allows.
  • To inform our thinking about LFH. We ask permission before naming or quoting you publicly and do not include patient information.

Emailing LFH does not subscribe you to updates. We do not sell your information, share it for marketing or add you to the sales lists of any commercial app.

Please don't send patient information

Describe the workflow, not a patient. Do not submit patient records or identifying medical details. If we find patient records or identifying medical details in a message, we will delete that material.

Asking us to delete it

To ask us to delete what you sent, email hello@localfirsthealth.org with the subject "Delete my information".

The record should travel. Not the patient.
