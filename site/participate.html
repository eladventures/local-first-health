Contact LFH

Start a conversation.

If these ideas matter to your work, we'd like to hear from you. Share a workflow problem, question or perspective.

hello@localfirsthealth.org

Email is the only way to reach LFH. LFH is an early-stage initiative that develops as time allows. We have limited capacity and may be slow to respond.

What you can send

  • The answer you got. If you asked a software provider what happens to your access when the subscription ends, email us the answer you got. Your experience may help us improve the wording. If you like, include what kind of software it is, the answer in the provider's own words, and whether it came in writing.
  • A moment from clinic work. What happened, and how do you work around it today?
  • A technical question or idea. What is missing or unworkable in the proposed principles?
  • A perspective on keeping records useful. You don't need a technical background to get in touch.
Describe the workflow, not a patient. Please do not send patient records or identifying medical details.

Contacting LFH does not subscribe you to updates or put you on a sales list. We ask permission before naming or quoting you publicly. See Privacy.

The record should travel. Not the patient.
